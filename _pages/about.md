I'm a Cognitive Science Ph.D. student in [Greene Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience Lab](https://greenelab.ucsd.edu/)(PI: Deanna Greene) at UC San Diego. I am particularly interested in using neuroimaging methods that can explain and predict **developmental trajectories** and **how our brain changes** across the life span. 


My research mainly focuses on fMRI and data-driven methodologies. Before joining Greene Lab, I studied cognitive neuroscience in [Human Affective Neuroscience (HumAN) Lab](https://www.affectiveneurosciencelab.com/)(PI: Justin Minue Kim) at Sungkyunkwan University, South Korea.

<br/>

Research Interests 
- biomarkers of neuropsychiatric disorders
- functional brain network organization
- predictive modeling, data clustering and integration
